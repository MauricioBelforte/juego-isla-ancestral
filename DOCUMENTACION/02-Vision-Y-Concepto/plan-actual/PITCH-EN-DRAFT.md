## Pitch EN (propuesta)

> "Isla Ancestral is a voxel life and building game where you arrive at an apparently empty island to start over. Beneath your shovel sleeps a civilization that learned to reshape the world... and every block you move, every temple you discover, brings you closer to something that perhaps should not have been awakened. No combat. No rush. Just your home, your neighbors, and a secret beneath the earth."

## Descripción de una frase (EN)

> "A cozy voxel building adventure where your home grows as you discover the civilization sleeping beneath your island."

## Tabla de sintagma clave ES → EN

| ES | EN | Notas |
|----|----|-------|
| Isla Ancestral | Isla Ancestral | Nombre propio, no se traduce |
| juego de vida y construcción voxel | voxel life and building game | "vida" → "life", "construcción" → "building" |
| isla aparentemente vacía | apparently empty island | "aparentemente" → "apparently" |
| empezar de nuevo | start over | phrasal verb natural |
| Bajo tu pala duerme una civilización | Beneath your shovel sleeps a civilization | "pala" → "shovel" (no "spade") |

| te acerca a algo que tal vez no debería haber sido despertado | brings you closer to something that perhaps should not have been awakened | "despertado" → "awakened" (no "woken") |
| Sin combate | No combat | conciso |
| Sin prisa | No rush | "prisa" → "rush" (más natural que "hurry") |
| Solo tu hogar, tus vecinos y un secreto bajo la tierra | Just your home, your neighbors, and a secret beneath the earth | "solo" → "just" (más cozy) |

## Notas de traducción
- "Isla Ancestral" se mantiene como nombre propio (no se traduce).
- "Resonancia" (concepto clave) se mantiene en español en el texto EN para mantener misterio.
- El tono cozy se preserva con "just", "no rush", "your home".
- "awakened" (no "woken") para mantener el tono solemne.
- "reshape" (no "modify") para mayor evocación.
